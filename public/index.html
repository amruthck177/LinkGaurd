FORENSIC HEURISTIC INDEX · 10 DETECTORS

Heuristic Reference

All detectors are deterministic, client-side, and zero-dependency. Each maps to a documented phisher tradecraft pattern.

01 — Protocol Security Transport · 15 pts

Modern services mandate TLS. Unencrypted http:// allows on-path interception and script injection. Missing scheme defaults to insecure transport.

02 — IP-Literal Host Domain · 35 pts

Legitimate organisations use branded domain names. Direct IPv4/IPv6 hosts like http://192.168.1.1/login bypass DNS reputation systems entirely — favoured by fast-turnaround exploit kits.

03 — @ Symbol Redirection Obfuscation · 35 pts

RFC defines @ as the credential-host separator. Attackers craft paypal.com@evil.com/login — the browser navigates to the real host after @, but users read the brand name before it.

04 — Punycode / Homoglyphs Visual Deception · 30 pts

IDNs translate non-Latin chars via xn--. Phishers register Cyrillic а or Greek ο that are visually indistinguishable from Latin. LinkGuard decodes all labels and normalises homoglyphs.

05 — Subdomain Depth Domain · 15 pts

Stacking 4+ subdomains buries the real registered domain off-screen on mobile. On a phone, users see the brand in the visible address bar while evil.com sits beyond the edge.

06 — Hyphen Density Visual Deception · 15 pts

Strings like apple-id-security-recovery.com mimic multi-word brand portals using hyphens to bypass trademark blocklists during registration.

07 — URL Shortener Mask Destination Obfuscation · 25 pts

Services like bit.ly, tinyurl.com, t.co cloak the real destination host. Used heavily in SMS phishing and spear-phishing campaigns to prevent pre-click inspection.

08 — Suspicious TLDs Domain · 20 pts

TLDs like .tk .ml .ga .cf .xyz .top .buzz offer free or near-free registration. They carry elevated historical cybercrime rates and are disproportionately favoured by phishing kits.

09 — Brand Impersonation Identity · 35 pts

Flags high-value brands (Google, PayPal, Apple, Chase, Microsoft, Amazon…) appearing in subdomains or paths when the root domain is NOT owned by that brand. Also catches Punycode-decoded homoglyph brand spoofing.

10 — Shannon Entropy Obfuscation · 15 pts

Shannon entropy > 4.2 bits/char in long URLs indicates base64 payloads, binary staging params, or randomised malware tokens. Legitimate URLs do not exhibit this pattern.
